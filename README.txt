THE ONE-PARAGRAPH BRIEF
-----------------------
Engagement is a content problem, not a frequency problem (June audit).
Publish the give, not the ask: the SkySlope Index (proprietary transaction
data nobody else has), testimonial videos (the only proven format), and
zero-CTA relatable content. Build the audience deliberately: owned-channel
follow prompts, employee advocacy, partner tag exchanges, community
participation as named humans. Never bot the DMs or follows — it's a ban
risk and the credibility we're rebuilding is the whole point.
